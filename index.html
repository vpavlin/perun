The name
Perun, god of thunder, guarded the Dark Forest. This app guards your trace.

In Slavic myth Perun rides the storm and keeps what is hidden, hidden. Modern running apps do the opposite — they hand your movements, your home, your habits to companies you'll never meet. Perun takes the old bargain back: your data is private by default, revealed only when you decide.

What it does

Record the run. Keep the run.

Live map, splits, pace, elevation and heart-rate — the detail you want from a tracker, without the surveillance you didn't ask for.

Run & ride

Live GPS with a map that draws itself, and a full breakdown after — distance, splits, pace, elevation, HR. Runs and bike rides alike.

Private by default

Every track stays on your phone and your paired devices. Nothing is uploaded to a third party. There is no account to make.

Sync over Logos

Runs travel peer-to-peer over Waku to the Perun Analytics module on your Basecamp desktop — a bigger screen for splits and history, still no server in the middle.

Proof-of-Run

Share a verified statistic from a run — the number, provably, without handing over the whole route. Your achievement, not your address.

Annotate the route

Drop photos along the way as you go. Each becomes a waypoint on the map — and a frame in your ride film.

Works offline

Out of signal in the hills? It records anyway and reconciles later. Local-first isn't a setting — it's the whole design.

New

Turn a ride into a film.

Perun renders a cinematic video of your ride — the route drawing itself across a dark map, your annotation photos rising full-frame as the dot passes them, a quick cross-dissolve between shots. It's built entirely on-device: no upload, no render farm, nothing leaves your phone.

Pick the size, hero the photos or tuck them in a card, and share the clip. The recording of a paused stretch even shows honestly, as a dashed bridge — because a film of your ride should be true to it.
